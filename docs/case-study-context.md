# BetXchange Case Study Context

## Business Scenario

BetXchange is an online and retail sports betting company operating primarily in South Africa, with plans to expand globally. The business offers a broad mix of sports betting, Lotto, Casino, and Betgames products.

The company wants to improve user engagement and profitability by using data-driven decisions. The business challenge is to understand customer behaviour, betting activity, financial performance, product performance, customer retention, and operational risk.

## Analysis Scope

The assessment is based on a three-month dataset covering May, June, and July 2024.

Business domains to analyse:
- user behaviour
- betting patterns and preferences
- financial performance and risk
- customer retention
- marketing performance
- operational efficiency
- strategic recommendations

## Core Assessment Tasks

1. User Behaviour Analysis
   - understand demographics and behaviour patterns
   - segment users by age, country, and engagement profile

2. Betting Patterns and Preferences
   - identify product and betting trends
   - understand active usage and betting intensity

3. Financial Transactions and Risk Assessment
   - trace revenue, deposits, withdrawals, and outcomes
   - identify abnormal patterns and risk indicators

4. Marketing Performance Evaluation
   - assess campaign impact and traffic-to-conversion quality
   - understand user acquisition and engagement quality

5. Operational Efficiency Analysis
   - identify friction points and operational bottlenecks
   - improve business controls and process design

6. Strategic Recommendations
   - propose data-driven actions for growth and engagement

7. Data Strategy Alignment
   - ensure the data practices support company goals and governance requirements
