What this page can and cannot tell you

Scope and limits

These figures are computed directly from cleaned.transactions and are fully reproducible (see docs/assumptions.md). Operator margin is stake minus payout, divided by stake — the business's gross gaming revenue as a % of volume, not a customer-facing "loss ratio". The large-stake threshold shown above is that month's own 95th percentile of transaction stake, not a fixed amount, so it adjusts as volume grows. This page cannot detect fraud, collusion, bonus abuse, or multi-accounting — the source data has no device, IP, payment method, or account-linkage fields. What it does show reliably: revenue is positive and growing, and a very small number of users account for a large share of stake and payout volatility — a concentration / dependency risk, not evidence of wrongdoing.
