Recency

Customers by latest activity month

Each customer is counted once, in the month of their MOST RECENT transaction -- a recency/churn signal. A customer whose last activity was in May and never returned shows up here under May, not July.

Retention

Repeat activity by cohort and activity month

Retention rate = customers from a registration-month cohort with a transaction in that activity month, divided by that cohort's size. Source: analytics.retention_kpis.

Coverage limitation

Age and country analysis covers 33% of customers only

Only 7,918 of 24,142 transacting customers (33%) have a matching row in the raw Users files. The remaining 16,224 customers (67%) placed bets but have no age or country on record at all -- the Users exports do not cover every customer who transacted. The two charts below describe only the matched 33% and must not be read as covering the full customer base. This coverage gap is itself a data-governance finding for Gracia. See docs/assumptions.md.
